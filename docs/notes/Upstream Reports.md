- 🩹 📋 Legendary Green Steel tier 1 — equipment guard: wiki 10d6, his augment 8d6. Weapon spell power: wiki Magnetism 139, his +150. Weapon stats: wiki +12, his +15. - 🩹 📋 Thunder-Forged — Shadow Construct: wiki Profane Repair Amplification +10, his augment 10%. Source: https://ddowiki.com/page/Thunder-Forged. Corrected: bonus type Profane, name and description; his bonus value was already 10.
